Bug-fix release: much less warble when transposing.

## Fixed
- **Pitch-shift warble.** The transposer used to blend two delayed copies of the audio all the time, so the level swung several times a second and the pitch sounded wobbly. It now plays one copy at a time and hands over in a short 20 ms crossfade. Offline measurements across −12…+12 semitones:
  - level wobble is 4–7× lower
  - the deepest momentary dip went from 14% to at least 47% of the normal level
  - pitch is accurate to 0.01 cent for −5…+7 semitones (within 16 cents at ±12)

## Install
Download `PocketTranspose-v0.1.1.apk` and open it on the phone. It updates v0.1.0 in place: same signing key, and your per-video settings are kept.

## Known limitations
- Each handover is a tiny phase jump, so very large shifts (around ±12) can still sound slightly grainy. A phase-vocoder/WSOLA-quality transposer needs audio processing on the audio thread, which YouTube's page security policy blocks for in-page scripts.
- Navigating back to YouTube search inside the page stops playback, as on YouTube mobile web.
