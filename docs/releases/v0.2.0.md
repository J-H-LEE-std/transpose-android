## New
- **Adjust from the notification.** The playback notification now has −/+ buttons, so you can change the sound without opening the app. The values update in the app's panel at once and are saved for the current video, just like the panel's own buttons.
  - Collapsed: key and speed
  - Expanded: key, speed and volume
- **Open source licenses screen.** Panel → **Licenses** shows PocketTranspose's license (BSD 2-Clause) and the bundled third-party components with their license (Apache-2.0).

## Licensing
PocketTranspose is now released under the BSD 2-Clause License. This also covers earlier versions, including v0.1.0 and v0.1.1. Each release also includes `LICENSE.txt`.

## Install
Download `PocketTranspose-v0.2.0.apk` and open it on the phone. It updates v0.1.0/v0.1.1 in place (same signing key) and keeps your per-video settings.

## Known limitations
- The notification buttons step values; there is no seek bar or slider in the notification.
- Very large key changes (around ±12) can still sound slightly grainy.
- Navigating back to YouTube search inside the page stops playback, as on YouTube mobile web.
